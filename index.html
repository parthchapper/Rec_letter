Before you write: what a US letter is for

If you usually write UK-style references, these are the differences that matter most.

  • Evidence over summaryReaders want two or three specific moments they can picture: what happened, what Parth did and why it mattered.
  • Comparisons carry weightSay how he compares with students you have taught across your career, and give the reason.
  • Character countsHow he treats classmates, handles setbacks and changes the room is read alongside his ability.
  • Your own voiceFirst person is expected. About one page is typical; Caltech says letters usually run about a page.
  • Skip the listColleges already have his activities and honours. Your letter is the one place they hear from someone who watched him learn.
  • Candour builds trustA real difficulty he worked through is more convincing than unbroken praise.

If you use AI, keep the substance yours. The master prompt drafts only from your answers and the verified facts. Please edit the draft into your own voice and make sure you stand behind every sentence.
